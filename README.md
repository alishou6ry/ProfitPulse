Project Overview

ProfitPulse is a web application designed to help small business owners manage their finances and monitor their business performance.

The platform allows users to track revenue and expenses, calculate break-even points, forecast cash flow, and convert currencies using live exchange rates.

Problem Statement

Many small businesses struggle to manage their finances because they rely on spreadsheets, manual calculations, or scattered financial records.

ProfitPulse aims to solve this problem by providing a simple, centralized financial dashboard.

Target Users

Small business owners, startups, freelancers, and entrepreneurs who need an easy way to monitor their financial performance.

Main Features

1. Business Dashboard - View financial summaries and business performance.
2. Expense Management - Record, categorize, and monitor expenses.
3. Revenue Tracking - Record sales and analyze income.
4. Break-Even Calculator - Calculate the sales required to cover business costs.
5. Cash Runway Forecast - Estimate future cash availability and test financial scenarios.
6. Currency Converter - Convert currencies using live exchange rates.
